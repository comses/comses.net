Terms of Use define the legal relationship with the service operator; the other documents address specific research, community, and operational responsibilities. The draft status of these proposals does not replace the privacy policy currently in effect.

## How these documents relate

The index does not incorporate other policies into the Terms by itself. Any contractual incorporation must be stated explicitly in the approved Terms, with the applicable versions and change process. Operational guidance in the [FAQ](/about/faq/), [peer review instructions](/reviews/), and publishing tutorials explains procedures; it should remain consistent with the approved policies.

## Discretion and transparency

Curatorial discretion remains available. Accepted departures from normal requirements must be justified and clearly visible on the affected public record, including their implications for access, reuse, or review. Protect confidential details while making the limitation understandable. Discretion does not authorize violations of rights, privacy protections, or applicable law.

## Ownership and revisions

The CoMSES team is the contact for this draft collection. Each policy needs an approved owner, effective date, and revision history before adoption. Material changes should explain their effect on existing accounts and deposits. Direct questions or requests for clarification to the [contact page](/about/contact/).
