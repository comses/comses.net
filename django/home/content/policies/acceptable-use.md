## Responsible use of the service

Proposed rules apply to accounts, submissions, community posts, APIs, and automated access. Use CoMSES Net in ways that support research and community participation. Do not impersonate others, submit deceptive metadata, send spam, distribute malicious code, harass users, or disclose information you are not authorized to share. Research code with potentially harmful behavior should be clearly identified and discussed with curators before submission.

## Security and automated access

Do not bypass access controls, attempt to retrieve private submissions, disrupt the service, or evade restrictions. Respect published access limits and contact the team before bulk operations likely to affect availability. Public availability does not remove licensing, privacy, or attribution obligations. See the [AI and automated processing policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/).

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the [contact page](/about/contact/); avoid posting exploit details or personal data in public discussions.

## Responses and reconsideration

Proposed responses include asking for corrections, restricting access, or removing harmful content. Responses should be proportionate to the risk and allow an explanation and reconsideration where feasible. Urgent protective action may precede notice. The responsible decision makers, reporting process, and appeal route must be approved before this policy is adopted.

Community interactions are also addressed by the [code of conduct](/about/policy/code-of-conduct/).
