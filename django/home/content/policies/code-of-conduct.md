## Community expectations

Proposed rules: treat participants with respect, welcome differing experience and perspectives, credit others' work, and keep criticism focused on evidence and ideas. These expectations cover CoMSES-hosted discussions, review interactions, and other activities organized under the CoMSES name.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, unwanted sexual attention, intimidation, and publication of private personal information are unacceptable. Disagreement about methods, findings, or policy should remain possible without personal attacks.

## Reporting and fair handling

Report concerns through the [contact page](/about/contact/). Avoid publishing private reports or identifying details without permission. Proposed handling: limit disclosure to people who need the information, consider immediate safety needs, hear relevant perspectives, and document proportionate decisions. Do not promise absolute confidentiality when a response may require disclosure.

Anyone handling a report should disclose conflicts and recuse themselves when appropriate. Retaliation against a person for raising a concern in good faith is unacceptable. Responses may include guidance, moderation, participation restrictions, or exclusion, with a route to reconsideration.

Before adoption, designate trained contacts, an alternative reporting route when those contacts are involved, and a review process for contested decisions. The [Acceptable Use policy](/about/policy/acceptable-use/) addresses service misuse.
