> Draft for community and governance review. This document has not been adopted.

## Access to CoMSES Net

Proposed policy: make the website, documentation, and participation processes accessible to people with disabilities. Provide meaningful headings, descriptive links, text alternatives for informative images, captions or transcripts for media, keyboard access, and readable presentation.

## Submitted research materials

Contributors should provide accessible documentation and explain interaction or platform requirements that may limit access. When an artifact cannot reasonably be made accessible, describe the limitation and provide an alternative explanation or representative results where possible. Curators may allow justified exceptions if the limitation and its effect on access are clearly visible.

## Reporting barriers

Use the [contact page](/about/contact/) to describe an accessibility barrier, the page or task affected, and a preferred way to receive a response. Share only the personal information needed to explain the issue. The team should work with the reporter to identify a practical alternative while addressing the barrier.

Before adoption, confirm an accessibility standard and target, responsibility for remediation, an accessible reporting alternative if the contact form is itself a barrier, and a process for reporting known limitations. This draft makes no claim of audited conformance or guaranteed response times.
