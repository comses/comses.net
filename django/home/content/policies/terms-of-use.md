## Purpose and scope

These Terms are intended to define the legal relationship between users and the operator of CoMSES Net. They are separate from the operational and community policies listed in the [policy index](/about/policy/).

The CoMSES Executive Board is the policy approval authority. Its delegated operators carry out operational responsibilities within their delegated authority. Provider exceptions, exceptional licensing appeals and final internal moderation appeals follow the Board approval routes described in the relevant drafts.

## Accounts and responsibility

Proposed terms: provide accurate account information, protect account credentials, and use only accounts and resources you are authorized to access. Contributors remain responsible for their submissions, including materials prepared using AI or other automated tools. Report suspected account compromise through the [contact page](/about/contact/).

## Submitted content and reuse

Contributors should retain ownership of their work. The service needs permission to host, preserve, display, distribute, and technically process deposited content; the scope and duration of that permission require approval before these Terms are adopted. This permission is distinct from the licenses that contributors choose for reuse of their work by others. See the [copyright and licensing policy](/about/policy/copyright-licensing/).

Public access does not transfer ownership. Reuse depends on the applicable component license and law, including copyright exceptions where they apply. These proposed Terms do not add a blanket AI-training prohibition to contributor licenses or withdraw permissions already granted. Community expectations about attribution, disclosure and responsible reuse are explained in the [AI policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/#permissions-and-community-expectations); they do not amend those licenses.

## CoMSES-operated AI processing

Proposed spam-screening scope: deterministic checks may operate on private drafts and public content within CoMSES; AI inference is limited to publicly visible content and public fields. Private drafts, confidential reviews and nonpublic account information are excluded from spam inference. Implementation changes are tracked separately; the [privacy draft](/about/data-privacy/#audit) distinguishes the proposed boundary from current behavior.

Proposed review choice: contributors may opt out of CoMSES-operated AI assessment and summarization through a checkbox on the peer review request. The [review policy](/about/policy/review-publication/#ai-assistance-and-your-review-request) describes the scope, disclosures and implementation requirements. This choice does not authorize training, change the deposit's license, or restrict independent third-party use after publication. Public accessibility does not itself place a work in the public domain.

Proposed service commitment: require inference providers to process submitted inputs and outputs privately, minimize content retention, and prohibit training or fine-tuning on content CoMSES sends for processing, including publicly available content, unless the CoMSES Executive Board approves a documented exception before use. The [AI policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/#provider-commitments) describes the requirements, exception records and limits. Delegated human operators explicitly approve provider terms and permitted uses. This draft does not establish that all current provider agreements have been verified, and it cannot guarantee that unrelated parties will not train on public deposits. The operator must approve the commitment before adoption; each provider requires agreement verification or an approved documented exception before use.

## Relationship to other policies

The policy index provides navigation; linking a policy does not by itself state that it is incorporated into these Terms. Before adoption, the operator must identify which policies, if any, form part of the agreement, their applicable versions, and how users will be notified of changes. Privacy disclosures remain in the [Data Protection and Privacy policy](/about/data-privacy/).

## Decisions required before adoption

These Terms are incomplete until the responsible operator approves provisions for:

- The contracting entity, contact address, eligibility, and any age restrictions.
- Account registration, acceptance of the Terms, and authority to act for others.
- The contributor grant needed for hosting, preservation, and processing, including what survives account closure or withdrawal.
- Authorization for service-operated AI processing, any user consent controls, and the scope and enforceability of provider commitments.
- Service availability, warranties and disclaimers, and limits on liability.
- Suspension, termination, notice, and opportunities to seek reconsideration.
- Governing law, dispute resolution, and any applicable mandatory rights.
- Effective dates, changes to the Terms, and explicit incorporation of policies.

No jurisdiction, liability limit, or transfer of ownership is established by this draft. [Contact the CoMSES team](/about/contact/) with questions.
