## Deposit and optional peer review

[CoMSES peer review](/reviews/) is optional. It examines execution, documentation, and code quality against baseline criteria. A successful review supports a peer review badge and a DOI under the existing process. It does not certify scientific validity or suitability for a particular use.

Request scientific peer review using the Request Peer Review button on the model detail page. The contact page and support email route for reconsidering automated moderation decisions serve a separate purpose.

Releases remain private during review. Publication locks the release files; metadata can still be corrected. Changes to published files require a new release. See the [review guidance](/reviews/) for the current procedure.

## AI assistance and your review request

Proposed review-request control: offer an unchecked checkbox labeled **"Do not use AI to assess or summarize this release during peer review."** Selecting it opts out of CoMSES-operated AI assessment and summarization for that review, including assistance used by reviewers. Human peer review remains available. Leaving it unchecked allows only the AI assistance disclosed for the requested review; it does not authorize model training or unrelated processing.

Before submission, explain which release materials may be processed, the purposes, providers, retention and human checks. Private release materials may be used for this disclosed review assistance when the contributor has not opted out and applicable permissions are in place. Confidential reviewer correspondence requires separate authorization. The [AI policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/#provider-commitments) governs provider requirements and Executive Board exceptions. Human reviewers remain accountable for the assessment.

Suggested accompanying notice: **"Publishing makes your release publicly accessible; it does not place it in the public domain or change its license. Other parties may collect public materials for AI training, summarization or other inference. Some uses may be permitted by the license or law; others may be challengeable. CoMSES cannot guarantee that collection or reuse will be prevented."** This review preference controls CoMSES review assistance and cannot control independent third-party use of public material. The notice grants no additional permissions to third parties and waives no contributor rights. See the [licensing guidance](/about/policy/copyright-licensing/#ai-reuse-and-the-limits-of-licensing), [Creative Commons' public-domain explanation](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#what-is-the-public-domain) and [AI guidance](https://creativecommons.org/cc-license-guidance/).

The checkbox and its enforcement are not implemented. Before offering AI review assistance, record the choice with the review request, make it visible to reviewers, and enforce it before sending material or running an assessment. Historical requests without a recorded choice do not authorize AI assistance. Define how contributors can change their choice and how pending work and existing outputs are handled. The separate [spam-screening boundary](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/#review-and-service-operated-processing) continues to apply.

## Review integrity

Proposed expectations: reviewers disclose conflicts of interest, protect private materials, and distinguish observed evidence from inference and unverified claims. Record what was checked, the release reviewed, material limitations, and the basis of the decision. Automated checks may assist review but do not substitute for an accountable human decision. Use of AI with private submissions is subject to the [AI policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/).

Accepted exceptions and their implications must be clearly visible on the public record. A badge must not imply that an exception was tested successfully or that scientific claims were validated. Decide before adoption whether review reports, reviewer identities, or decision summaries will be public; do not release private review correspondence without authorization.

A public metadata record for restricted model materials does not by itself establish that the model was executed, reviewed or preserved by CoMSES. State which materials reviewers could access and what they checked; any badge or review statement must reflect those limits.

## Corrections, withdrawal, and disputes

Requests concerning published records should go to the [CoMSES team](/about/contact/). The [FAQ](/about/faq/) describes the current approach to removal. Proposed policy: distinguish metadata corrections, new releases, and exceptional withdrawal; document the reason for action without exposing protected information. Preserve citation continuity where feasible and lawful.

Before adoption, define who decides disputes and withdrawals, how affected parties can respond or seek reconsideration, and what notice remains at the record URL. Do not promise deletion of all distributed or archived copies.
