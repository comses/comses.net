## Deposit and optional peer review

[CoMSES peer review](/reviews/) is optional. It examines execution, documentation, and code quality against baseline criteria. A successful review supports a peer review badge and a DOI under the existing process. It does not certify scientific validity or suitability for a particular use.

Releases remain private during review. Publication locks the release files; metadata can still be corrected. Changes to published files require a new release. See the [review guidance](/reviews/) for the current procedure.

## Review integrity

Proposed expectations: reviewers disclose conflicts of interest, protect private materials, and distinguish observed evidence from inference and unverified claims. Record what was checked, the release reviewed, material limitations, and the basis of the decision. Automated checks may assist review but do not substitute for an accountable human decision. Use of AI with private submissions is subject to the [AI policy](/about/policy/ai-automated-processing/).

Accepted exceptions and their implications must be clearly visible on the public record. A badge must not imply that an exception was tested successfully or that scientific claims were validated. Decide before adoption whether review reports, reviewer identities, or decision summaries will be public; do not release private review correspondence without authorization.

## Corrections, withdrawal, and disputes

Requests concerning published records should go to the [CoMSES team](/about/contact/). The [FAQ](/about/faq/) describes the current approach to removal. Proposed policy: distinguish metadata corrections, new releases, and exceptional withdrawal; document the reason for action without exposing protected information. Preserve citation continuity where feasible and lawful.

Before adoption, define who decides disputes and withdrawals, how affected parties can respond or seek reconsideration, and what notice remains at the record URL. Do not promise deletion of all distributed or archived copies.
